RAN AUTOMATION & RESILIENCE LAB - v2.6.0
AI-GATED CLOSED LOOP + SAFETY CHECKPOINT + CIRCUIT BREAKER
============================================================

CIL
---
v2.6 zachovava deterministicky v2.4 optimizer jako autoritu pro fyziku,
candidate generation, guardrails a objective scoring. AI NESMI menit target cell,
parameter ani target value. AI smi vratit pouze:

APPROVE / HOLD / ABSTAIN

Pokud AI vrati APPROVE, samostatny deterministicky supervisor muze poslat PRESNE
ten optimizer candidate pres existujici guarded_apply(). Zmena se nepovazuje za
plne overenou okamzite. Pred apply se ulozi posledni verified-healthy checkpoint.
Po apply musi projit okamzita health kontrola a pak dalsi observation cycle.

FAIL / UNHEALTHY po AI zmene:
-> force restore predchoziho verified-healthy checkpointu
-> verify rollback
-> bad-AI outcome strike

5 po sobe jdoucich bad AI-approved outcomes:
-> circuit breaker OPEN
-> dalsi AI-gated actuation DISABLED
-> optimizer zustava read-only dostupny
-> manual reset je povolen jen kdyz je aktualni RAN healthy

Provider timeout / missing key / malformed AI output / HOLD / ABSTAIN:
-> zadny state-changing call
-> strike se NEPOCITA

V2.6 auto-actuator allowlist:
- tx_power_dbm
- electrical_tilt_deg

TRAFFIC_STEERING zustava mimo AI auto-actuation a patri do oddelene capacity
recovery / policy cesty.

Default loop interval:
AI_CONTROL_INTERVAL_SECONDS=60
AI_CONTROL_BAD_DECISION_THRESHOLD=5

Supervisor neprekryva cykly. Pokud kompletni optimizer + AI + verification trva
dele nez 60 s, interval zvys. Status vystavuje duration_seconds a interval_overrun.

INTERVIEW-SAFE ARCHITECTURE SENTENCE
====================================

"The deterministic optimizer still owns the physics, candidate generation,
hard guardrails and target value. The AI is a bounded decision gate that can
only approve, hold or abstain on that exact candidate. A separate supervisor
performs guarded apply, verifies the next observation window, restores the
previous verified-healthy checkpoint on failure, and opens a circuit breaker
after five consecutive bad AI-approved outcomes. Direct model-to-RAN
actuation is disabled."


CO JE DULEZITE NECLAIMOVAT
==========================

- Neni to production T-Mobile RAN.
- Neni to production Kubernetes/RAN experience.
- AI nema prime vendor/O-RAN radio credentials.
- LLM neprepocitava RF fyziku a neurcuje libovolne TX/tilt hodnoty.
- 60 s neni univerzalni produkcni RAN control-loop SLA; je to learning-lab
  observation period. Produkcni interval by se ridil use-casem, telemetry
  freshness, settling time, interface latency a operational policy.
- Circuit-breaker state je v tomto labu in-memory. Production by vyzadovala
  persistence, audit, idempotency, leader election/coordination a HA state.
